## First interface boundary

The October 2022 tile archives and their index can supply a historical observation viewer. Use the acquisition date and water-context legend supplied with each archive. The wider warning product requires separately collected outcome evidence. The spec places the first map interface before national multi-season inference and the operational daily risk model.

## Forecast data contract

One record represents one LGA and one issue time. Supply timezone-aware issue, window start and exclusive end, availability times for the forecast, antecedent and static inputs, source identifiers, a versioned susceptibility estimate on a 0–1 scale, built-up fraction, preceding 1/3/7/14-day rain, next 24/48/72-hour forecast rain and maximum hourly rain. Keep the underlying forecast response as retrieved. For training, record the reviewer, evidence ID, observation interval and evidence kind. A reviewed onset interval has to fit inside the horizon. An event-free record must cover the entire horizon. An absent report has an unknown outcome.

The research training command is `python -m floodrisk.train_event --source windows.csv --output model_dir`. It reserves 2022 for isotonic calibration and 2023 onward for testing, drops windows crossing split boundaries and rejects evidence reused across partitions. The scoring entry point is `python -m jobs.daily_score --inputs issue.csv --registry reports/hotspots_oct22/lgas.csv --model-dir model_dir --output scores.csv`. A synthetic integration check exercised 774 rows and discarded its temporary outputs. Real training and daily risk scores are pending. A forecast archive assembled from the initial hours of successive runs cannot reproduce the full 72-hour forecast available on an earlier date. [Open-Meteo historical archive documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api)

## Review on 25 November 2026

- **Historical maps:** re-read both sets of eight PMTiles archives, verified file hashes and decoded all 356 PNG tiles. The national registry contains 774 LGAs; the October observations supply partial footprints in 22.
- **Segmentation:** independently summed per-chip intersections and unions for both sensors on 89 validation, 90 test and 15 Bolivia chips. The results match the saved evaluation. The 48 reserved Nigerian chips still require independent masks. A dated optical search found 30 chips with zero qualifying clear pixels even in their best matching Sentinel-2 scene. [Review packet](reports/reserved_nigeria/README.md)
- **Rainfall and radar:** durable forecast refresh and daily map generation operate on GitHub. The scheduled radar job and a new health check succeeded following the earlier download-permission failure. [Scheduled execution](https://github.com/okiv21/Flood-Risk-Mapper/actions/runs/45607322335) · [Freshness verification](https://github.com/okiv21/Flood-Risk-Mapper/actions/runs/45661443098)
- **Forecast evaluation:** six original ECMWF runs were paired with two reported floods. A separate Onitsha South benchmark evaluated fixed rules against a satellite target using 24 radar acquisitions and 48 GFS forecasts. Independent recalculation from the retrieved results reproduced all six held-out confusion tables. Each rule missed all three above-threshold dates at each lead. The national warning model still lacks independently observed event and event-free periods and demonstrated warning skill. [Whole-LGA benchmark](reports/satellite_replay/README.md#held-out-results)
- **Terrain and delivery:** the spatial and temporal partition checks passed. The terrain model's recorded wider-area and restricted Lokoja scores differ materially. National terrain inference and independent field validation remain open. The first historical viewer can use the saved map packages. Application development remains outside this stage.

The [sealed 48-chip packet](https://github.com/okiv21/Flood-Risk-Mapper/releases/tag/blind-nigeria-review-v1) can be downloaded for independent annotation. The [outcome evidence form](reports/event_replay/outcome_evidence_template.csv) records dated flood and monitored event-free intervals. These are separate evidence needs: a water mask can evaluate segmentation accuracy on its date, while warning evaluation requires independently timed outcomes across a forecast horizon.
